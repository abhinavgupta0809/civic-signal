The Transportation Department outlined a plan on Wednesday to distribute $1.8 billion in grants for bridge repairs across 30 states over the next three years. Officials said the funds come from a previously approved infrastructure package and will prioritize structures rated in poor condition. A department official said states must submit applications by the end of the fiscal year and provide matching funds for larger projects. The plan sets aside a portion of the money for rural counties. An independent transportation analyst said the amounts were broadly consistent with prior funding rounds. The department said the first grants would be awarded within six months and that a full list of eligible projects would be published online.
