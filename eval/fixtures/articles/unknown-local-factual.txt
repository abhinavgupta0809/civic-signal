The city council voted 5 to 2 on Tuesday to approve funding for repairs to the Maple Street bridge, which has been closed to heavy trucks since a March inspection found corrosion on two support beams. The approved budget allocates $2.4 million for the work, with construction expected to begin in September and finish before winter. The city engineer said a detour will remain in place during the repairs. Two council members who voted against the measure said they wanted more competitive bids before committing the funds. A public comment period drew about a dozen residents, most of whom supported the project.
