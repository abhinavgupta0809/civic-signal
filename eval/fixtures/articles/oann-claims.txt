A new report circulating among administration critics alleges that a senior federal official secretly funneled contracts to allies, though the outlet provided no documents to support the claim. The report attributes its information to "a source familiar with the matter" and does not name the official involved. No other news organization has confirmed the allegations, and the agency in question flatly denied any wrongdoing when reached. Commentators quickly amplified the story on social media as proof of deep corruption. The outlet said additional bombshells are forthcoming. No independent evidence was offered, and the accused official's office called the claims "completely fabricated."
