Leaders from 20 economies concluded a two-day summit on Saturday with a joint statement pledging to coordinate on trade and climate financing. The communique, released by the host government, said members agreed to review tariff barriers over the next year but set no binding targets. Three officials briefed on the talks said negotiations over the final language continued late into the evening. A spokesperson for the host delegation confirmed the statement was adopted by consensus. Analysts noted the language was less specific than last year's agreement. The next summit is scheduled to be held in the spring, according to the host government.
