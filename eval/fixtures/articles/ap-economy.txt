The U.S. Labor Department reported Friday that employers added 187,000 jobs in June, while the unemployment rate held steady at 4.1%. Average hourly earnings rose 0.3% from the previous month. Economists surveyed before the release had expected a gain of roughly 190,000 jobs. Revisions lowered the prior two months' totals by a combined 49,000. The report is one of several indicators the Federal Reserve weighs ahead of its next policy meeting. A Labor Department official said the figures are subject to revision as more survey responses are collected. Hiring was strongest in health care and hospitality, the agency said.
