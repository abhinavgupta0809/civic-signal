A regional rail operator said on Thursday it will add 12 new services on its busiest commuter line starting in September, following months of complaints about overcrowding. The company said the extra trains would increase capacity on the route by roughly 15% during peak hours. A transport authority spokesperson confirmed the schedule had been approved and said fares would not rise as a result of the change. The operator said it had recruited and trained additional drivers to run the services. Passenger groups cautiously welcomed the announcement but said reliability, not just capacity, remained the bigger concern on the line during the winter months.
