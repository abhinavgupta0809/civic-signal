The governor's sweeping new budget proposal landed with a thud among members of her own party on Thursday, with several lawmakers privately grumbling that the plan asks too much, too fast. The proposal would raise spending on transit by 12% while trimming a handful of tax credits. Supporters call it a bold reset; critics say it papers over a looming deficit. Two legislative aides, speaking on condition of anonymity, described the rollout as chaotic. A spokesperson for the governor defended the plan as fiscally responsible and said the numbers add up. Whether it can survive the coming committee fight is anyone's guess.
