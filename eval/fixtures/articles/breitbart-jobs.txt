A manufacturing company announced Tuesday that it will open a new facility in the state, a project it says will create about 600 jobs over the next two years. Company executives said construction will begin in the fall, with the plant expected to produce components for electric vehicles. State officials who attended the announcement said the company qualified for a standard economic-development tax incentive tied to hiring targets. The firm reported $4.2 billion in revenue last year. A company spokesperson said hiring for the first roles would begin in early spring. Local officials welcomed the investment, pointing to the region's recent manufacturing job losses and its push to attract new employers.
