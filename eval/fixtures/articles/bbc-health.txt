A study published in a peer-reviewed medical journal this week found that adults who walked at least 7,000 steps a day had a lower risk of early death than those who walked fewer. The research followed roughly 15,000 participants over eight years. The authors cautioned that the study was observational and could not prove that walking directly caused the reduced risk. Independent researchers not involved in the work said the findings were consistent with earlier evidence but called for randomized trials. A co-author said the results should not be read as a precise daily target. Funding came from a national health research council.
