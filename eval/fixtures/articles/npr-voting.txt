With the primary approaching, election officials in several states are reminding voters of registration deadlines and the rules for mail-in and absentee ballots. Officials said polling places will open at 7 a.m. on election day, and that voters already registered do not need to re-register. A state elections spokesperson said requests for absentee ballots must be received at least seven days before the vote. Voting-rights groups have set up hotlines to answer questions about voter ID requirements. Officials urged residents to confirm their polling place online, noting that some locations changed after redistricting. Provisional ballots remain available for voters whose eligibility is in question.
